# mary heart orbita


**Mary Heart Orbita — Digital Portfolio**

A clean, modern personal portfolio website for Mary Heart Orbita, a 4th-year BSIT student at Cebu Eastern College. The site presents her academic journey, growing technical skills, and creative projects through an elegant single-page layout.

**Design & Aesthetic**
The page blends editorial sophistication with playful, youthful energy. It pairs the refined serif of *Playfair Display* with the approachable sans-serif of *DM Sans*, set against soft ambient glows and delicate orbiting visual elements. Floating cards, sparkles, and subtle motion cues give the design a light, approachable personality without sacrificing professionalism.

**Key Sections**
- **Hero** — A warm "Hello, world!" introduction with a framed portrait, floating accent cards, and a clear call to explore her work.
- **About** — A personal narrative paired with a facts panel covering her year level, school, interests, and mindset.
- **Skills** — Four skill cards (Web Foundations, Problem Solving, Creative Thinking, Collaboration) with illustrative icons and descriptive tags.
- **Projects** — A featured project grid showcasing a portfolio concept plus placeholders for UI design and programming work.
- **Hobbies** — A personal touch highlighting music, creativity, café moments, and family time.
- **Contact** — A friendly closing invitation to connect, with a direct email call-to-action.
- **Footer** — A branded sign-off with an auto-updating copyright year.

**Technical Highlights**
- Fully responsive layout with a collapsible mobile navigation menu.
- Semantic HTML5 structure with accessible aria labels and alt text.
- Google Fonts integration and organized external CSS/JS files.
- Graceful image fallback: a styled placeholder appears automatically if the profile photo fails to load.
- Custom CSS animations for orbits, sparkles, and ambient glows.

**Purpose**
The portfolio serves as a personal introduction and a learning showcase — positioning Mary Heart as a curious, creative, and motivated future IT professional while providing a flexible template she can update with real projects and contact details before sharing.
